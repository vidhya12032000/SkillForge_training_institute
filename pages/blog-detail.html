How to Improve Your Typing Speed and Accuracy

Learn simple and practical techniques to improve your typing speed, reduce errors and become more productive while working on a computer.

SkillForge Team Learning & Career Team
September 15, 2026
6 min read

Typing is one of the most useful computer skills for students, job seekers and professionals. Improving your typing speed is not only about typing faster — accuracy, consistency and proper finger placement are equally important.

Why Is Typing Speed Important?

A good typing speed helps you complete everyday computer tasks faster. Writing emails, preparing documents, entering data, coding and searching for information all become easier when you can type comfortably.

However, speed should not be your only goal. A high number of words per minute with many mistakes can reduce productivity. The better approach is to improve speed and accuracy together.

Remember

Accuracy first, then gradually increase your typing speed.

1. Learn Proper Finger Placement

Proper finger placement is the foundation of touch typing. Your fingers should start from the home row and each finger should learn the keys it is responsible for.

Q W E R T Y U I O P
A S D F G H J K L
Z X C V B N M

2. Practice Every Day

Regular practice is more effective than practising for several hours only once a week. Even 10 to 20 minutes of focused typing practice every day can help you develop consistency.

  • Practice for a fixed amount of time every day.
  • Focus on accuracy before speed.
  • Gradually increase the difficulty.
  • Track your WPM and accuracy.

3. Don't Look at the Keyboard

Touch typing means typing without constantly looking at the keyboard. Initially this may feel slower, but with practice your fingers will start remembering the key positions.

4. Focus on Accuracy

Making fewer mistakes can make you more productive than simply trying to increase your WPM. Slow down whenever necessary and build muscle memory through repetition.

5. Measure Your Progress

Testing your typing speed regularly helps you understand whether your practice is working. Record your WPM, accuracy and errors and compare your results over time.

Conclusion

Improving typing speed is a gradual process. Instead of focusing only on speed, build proper technique, practise consistently and maintain good accuracy.

With regular practice, typing can become a natural skill that saves time and improves your overall computer productivity.
