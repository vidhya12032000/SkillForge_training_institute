Simple Typing Tips

Use All Fingers

Practice proper finger placement instead of typing with only a few fingers.

Look at the Screen

Try not to look at the keyboard. Build muscle memory through regular practice.

Focus on Accuracy

Speed will naturally improve when you become comfortable typing accurately.

Practice Daily

Even 10 minutes of daily typing practice can help build consistency.
